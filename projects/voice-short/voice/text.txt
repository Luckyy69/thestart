That voice you hear on recordings? Bad news. That's the real you. When you talk, you hear yourself twice. Once through the air, like everyone else. And once through your own skull. Your bones carry the sound straight to your ears, and make it deeper. A microphone only catches the air part. So that weird voice is the one everyone has heard... your whole life. But here's the twist. In one study, people rated a bunch of recorded voices, without knowing theirs was in there. Their own voice? They rated it higher than everyone else's. You don't hate your voice. You just don't recognize it.
