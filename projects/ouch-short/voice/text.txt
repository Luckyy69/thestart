Ever hit your toe and yelled "OW!" before you even knew what happened? Your brain is fast. But it might also be a little dramatic. A pain signal races from your toe to your brain in a split second, and your mouth reacts before you can even think. And here's the weird part: that "ow" actually makes it hurt less. In English, it's "ow." In French, "aïe." In Spanish, "ay." In Danish, "av." Scientists checked a hundred and thirty-one languages. A surprising number share the same "ah" sound. Then a team in Singapore tried something mean. They made people hold their hand in ice water, as long as they could. The people who said "ow" lasted about twenty percent longer. Hearing someone else say "ow"? Didn't help. You have to say it yourself. But another team found something even stronger. Swearing. People who swore held on about a third longer. Made-up swear words? Didn't work. And people who swear all day? They get less of a boost. So save your swear words... for your toes.
