# "The War Australia Lost to Birds" (the Great Emu War, 1932) — script v1
Facts (sources: Wikipedia "Emu War"; Britannica "Emu War"; National Geographic; History Hit; Australian Geographic):
- After WWI many ex-soldiers farmed wheat in Western Australia (Campion district); ~20,000 emus migrated in and ate/trampled the crops.
- Nov 1932: the army sent Major G.P.W. Meredith, two soldiers (Sgt McMurray, Gunner O'Halloran), two Lewis machine guns, 10,000 rounds.
- An ambush at a dam on ~1,000 emus: the gun jammed after about a dozen were killed.
- A gun mounted on a truck: the truck could not keep up with the birds (emus run ~50 km/h) on the rough ground.
- The emus split into small groups, each with a lookout bird; the army withdrew; 986 confirmed kills overall.
- Meredith: if he had a division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds it would face any army in the world ("...the invulnerability of tanks").

## Voice-over
In 1932, Australia declared war on birds. And lost.
After World War One, thousands of soldiers became wheat farmers in Western Australia.
Then twenty thousand emus showed up and ate everything.
So the farmers called the army.
The army sent one major, two soldiers, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets.
Easy, right?
First ambush: a thousand emus at a watering hole. The gun jams after twelve.
So they bolt a machine gun onto a truck. The emus run fifty kilometers an hour. The truck can't keep up.
Then the birds split into small groups, each with its own lookout.
Basically, guerrilla warfare.
After weeks of fighting, the army goes home.
The major said, if he had soldiers who could take bullets like these birds, he could beat any army in the world.
It's still called the Great Emu War.
Final score: Emus, one. Army, zero.
